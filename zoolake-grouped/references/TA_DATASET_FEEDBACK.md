# TA feedback on the dataset

The following is a transcription of the feedback addressed to Can, supplied to the project in a screenshot. The screenshot contains no visible date.

> Dear Can,
>
> Since GalaxyMNIST is derived from DECaLS images, it still conflicts with the other group. I can suggest this dataset to you:
>
> ZooLake Plankton Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/project-oceania/zoolake
>
> It has a different domain but a similar task. Also, the classes are imbalanced; therefore, you should use a proper evaluation metric (such as the F1 score) in your experiments.
>
> You can also suggest datasets to me if you don't prefer to work on this one. I will be waiting for your decision.

The team adopted ZooLake and chose macro-F1 as its primary metric. Approval of the three-minute capture-group split is still a separate question for the TA.
